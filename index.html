Warm-up and Core Skills

1. In a right-angled triangle, sin theta = 5/13 and 0 degrees < theta < 90 degrees. Find theta to the nearest degree.

2. Without a calculator, find the exact value of tan 45 degrees + sin 30 degrees.

3. Triangle ABC has vertices A(0, 0), B(18, 0), and C(18, 12). It is right-angled at B. Find angle BAC to the nearest degree.

4. A 4.8 m access ramp rises 0.72 m vertically. Find the angle the ramp makes with the ground, to the nearest tenth of a degree.

5. A kite string is taut and makes an angle of 58 degrees with level ground. The person holding it is 32 m horizontally from the point directly below the kite. Ignore the holder's height. How high is the kite above the ground, to the nearest metre?

6. A 7.5 m support cable runs from the top of a vertical radio mast to a ground anchor. The cable makes an angle of 62 degrees with the ground. Find the height of the mast to the nearest tenth of a metre.

7. On a scale drawing, a right triangle has a horizontal side of 6.4 cm and an angle of 37 degrees between this side and the hypotenuse. The real horizontal side is 160 m. Find the real vertical height, to the nearest metre.

8. Two points on level ground are 24 m apart. From the nearer point, the angle of elevation to the top of a statue is 41 degrees. From the farther point, it is 29 degrees. Explain which angle should be used to find the statue's height if the nearer point is used, then find that height to the nearest metre.

9. A solar panel is 1.7 m long. To meet its mounting specification, its top edge must be 0.95 m above its bottom edge. Find the panel's angle to the horizontal, to the nearest degree.

Reasoning and Challenge

10. A student writes cos 38 degrees = h/12 to find the height h of a vertical sign. The 12 m measurement is along the ground from the observer to the base of the sign, and the angle of elevation is 38 degrees.

Is the student's equation correct? If not, write a correct equation and find h to the nearest tenth of a metre.

11. An isosceles triangular window has two equal sides of 2.6 m and a base of 3.0 m. Draw the altitude from the top vertex. Use trigonometry to find the angle at the top vertex, to the nearest degree.

12. Challenge: A weather balloon is directly above a point on level ground. From point P, the angle of elevation is 28 degrees. After walking 45 m straight towards the point below the balloon to point Q, the angle of elevation is 46 degrees.

Let x be the horizontal distance from Q to the point below the balloon. Set up two equations involving x and the balloon height, then find the height to the nearest metre.
