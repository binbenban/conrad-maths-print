Robot Delivery Game
Division Mission

Use LEGO, coins, counters, or drawings as “energy cubes”. You will also need a six-sided die.

1. Build the delivery pads

Put 12 energy cubes equally onto 3 robot landing pads.

How many cubes are on each pad?

2. Robot code

Complete: 3 × ___ = 12

So: 12 ÷ 3 = ___

3. Pack the batteries

Put 20 batteries into packs of 5.

How many packs can you make? Draw the packs.

4. Choose the right spell

A robot has 24 bolts and shares them equally among 4 robots.

Circle the equation that matches:

24 + 4 24 − 4 24 ÷ 4 24 × 4

5. Dice delivery

Roll a die. Use that number of teams—but if you roll 5, roll again.

Share 24 cubes equally between the teams. How many does each team get?

6. Two ways to say it

What is the difference between:

“24 cubes shared among 4 robots”

“24 cubes packed in groups of 4”?

Solve both.

7. Spot the robot’s mistake

Robot Zed says: “18 shared among 3 means 18 − 3.”

Is Zed right? Show or explain the correct maths.

8. Remainder rescue

You have 23 repair kits. Each robot needs 4 kits.

How many robots can get a full set? How many kits are left?

9. Build the multiplication bridge

Fill in the missing numbers:

4 × ___ = 28

28 ÷ 4 = ___

28 ÷ ___ = 4

10. Choose a strategy

Solve 36 ÷ 4. You may use:

equal groups

a drawing

skip-counting

a multiplication fact

Which strategy did you choose?

11. Double the delivery

Four robots get 6 cubes each. Then the number of robots doubles.

If each robot still gets 6 cubes, how many cubes are needed now?

12. Invent a robot mission

Create a division challenge for a robot using cubes, LEGO, dice, or a drawing.

Include an equal-sharing or grouping problem, then solve it.
