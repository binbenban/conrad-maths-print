Trigonometry and Simultaneous Equations

Show working. Use the supplied diagrams, which are not to scale. Give answers to the requested accuracy and include units. Do not round during intermediate steps.

1. Exact trig ratios

For an acute angle θ, sin θ = 5/13. Without a calculator, find (a) cos θ and (b) tan θ.

2. Cinema tickets

At a cinema, 2 adult tickets and 3 student tickets cost $54. Three adult tickets and 2 student tickets cost $61. Find the cost of one adult ticket and one student ticket.

3. Ladder

A 7.8 m ladder leans against a vertical wall. The ladder makes an angle of 63° with the level ground.

(a) Find how high up the wall the ladder reaches, to the nearest tenth of a metre.

(b) Find the horizontal distance from the foot of the ladder to the wall, to the nearest tenth of a metre.

4. Coordinate trigonometry

Points A(−5, 2), B(7, 2), and C(7, 11) form a right-angled triangle.

(a) Find AC. (b) Find ∠BAC, to the nearest tenth of a degree.

5. Distance and bearing

Point B is 9 km east and 12 km north of point A.

(a) Find the straight-line distance from A to B. (b) Find the true bearing of B from A.

6. Cosine rule and area

Triangle ABC has AB = 16 cm, AC = 23 cm, and ∠BAC = 58°.

(a) Find BC, to the nearest tenth of a centimetre. (b) Find the area of triangle ABC, to the nearest square centimetre.

7. Bearings, cosine rule, and sine rule

From lighthouse L, boat A is on a bearing of 028° at a distance of 18 km. Boat B is on a bearing of 117° at a distance of 25 km.

(a) Find the distance between boats A and B. (b) Find the bearing of B from A.

8. Three-dimensional trigonometry

A rectangular storage crate is 15 cm long, 9 cm wide, and 12 cm high.

(a) Find the length of the space diagonal, to the nearest tenth of a centimetre.

(b) Find the angle between the space diagonal and the base of the crate, to the nearest tenth of a degree.

9. Simultaneous equations with two cables

A vertical mast is held by two taut cables attached to the ground on opposite sides. One cable makes an angle of 57° with the ground and the other makes an angle of 38° with the ground. The ground anchors are 42 m apart. Let x be the horizontal distance from the mast to the 57° anchor.

(a) Write two expressions for the mast height. (b) Form and solve an equation to find the mast height, to the nearest tenth of a metre. (c) Find the length of each cable.

10. Simultaneous equations with bearings

A hiker walks x km from point H on a bearing of 035°, then walks y km on a bearing of 142°. They finish 20 km due east of H.

(a) Resolve both journeys into east-west and north-south components. (b) Form simultaneous equations for x and y. (c) Find x and y, to the nearest tenth of a kilometre.
