Fractions — Share and Compare

Draw or explain your thinking.

7. Share the sandwiches

Three children share two identical sandwiches equally.
Draw the sandwiches and show how you would cut and share them so every child gets the same amount. There must be no sandwich left over.

You do not need to write fraction symbols.

8. Does “half” always mean the same amount?

Mia has a large rectangular cake. Leo has a much smaller rectangular cake.

Each cuts their own cake into two equal pieces and takes one piece.
Must their pieces be the same size? Draw an example to explain.
