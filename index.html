Measurement

You’ll need a ruler, paper and 12 small objects. Draw or explain your thinking where helpful.

1 — Measure, then slide

Draw a line 6 cm long. Measure it with your ruler starting at 0.
Now slide the ruler so the line starts at the 2 cm mark. Which mark does the other end reach? Has the line’s length changed?

2 — Find the starting mark

A ribbon is 7 cm long. One end is at the 12 cm mark on a ruler. The ribbon stretches towards 0.
At which mark is its other end? Draw to explain.

5 — Match the shaded amount

Draw two identical rectangular strips.
- Divide the first into 4 equal parts and shade 2 parts.
- Divide the second into 2 equal parts.

Shade the same amount on the second strip. Explain how you know the shaded amounts match.

6 — Half of a collection

Put 12 objects into two equal groups. One group is half of the collection.
Now put all 12 objects into four equal groups. How many of these smaller groups make half of the original collection? Show it with your objects.
