Trigonometry: Choose, Sketch, Check

Show a labelled sketch whenever it helps. Give answers to the requested accuracy and include units. Do not round during intermediate steps.

1

For an acute angle θ, tan θ = 1. State θ.

2

Without using a calculator, evaluate 2 cos 60° + tan 45°.

3

A right triangle has hypotenuse 17 cm and one shorter side 8 cm. Find the angle opposite the 8 cm side, to the nearest degree.

4

On a coordinate grid, R = (−6, 2), S = (4, 2), and T = (4, 9). Triangle RST is right-angled at S.

Find ∠SRT, to the nearest tenth of a degree.

5

A drone is hovering directly above a marked point on flat ground. From an observer, the angle of elevation is 34°. The observer is 27 m from the marked point.

(a) Find the drone’s height, to the nearest tenth of a metre.

(b) Another student says the height must be more than 27 m because the angle is greater than 30°. Is that claim reasonable? Explain briefly.

6

A surveyor views the top of a building from a balcony. The balcony floor is 18 m above ground, and the angle of depression from the balcony floor to the building’s base is 22°. The building is on level ground with the balcony.

Find the horizontal distance from the balcony to the building, to the nearest metre.

7

A triangular shade sail is an isosceles triangle with equal sides 6.8 m and base 8.0 m. A support pole is placed at the top vertex, perpendicular to the base at its midpoint.

Use trigonometry to find the angle between an equal side and the base, to the nearest degree.

8

A student is finding x, the length of the sloping side of a right triangle. The angle between the 14 m side and x is 53°. They write: sin 53° = 14/x.

Decide whether the equation is correct. If it is not, write a correct equation and find x to the nearest tenth of a metre.

9

A wheelchair-accessible path must rise 0.60 m. The path may be no steeper than 1 m vertical rise for every 14 m of horizontal run.

(a) What is the minimum horizontal run needed?

(b) If the path is built at that limiting gradient, find its length, to the nearest tenth of a metre.

10

A rectangular screen is 2.4 m wide. Its diagonal is 3.0 m.

(a) Find its height.

(b) Find the angle the diagonal makes with the horizontal, to the nearest degree.

11 — Challenge

From point A, the angle of elevation to the top of a tower is 18°. After walking 80 m straight towards the tower to point B, the angle of elevation is 31°. The ground is level.

Let x be the horizontal distance from B to the tower.

(a) Write two expressions for the tower height in terms of x.

(b) Use them to find the tower height, to the nearest metre.

12 — Challenge

A right triangular garden has one acute angle of 39°. The side adjacent to this angle is 12 m. A 1.2 m-wide strip is added outside the garden along the side opposite the 39° angle only.

Find the area of the added strip, to the nearest tenth of a square metre.
